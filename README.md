## 🐭 Mouzi

[![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/hsr88/mouzi?style=flat-square&logo=github&label=Stars)](https://github.com/hsr88/mouzi/stargazers)
[![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/hsr88/mouzi?style=flat-square&label=Release)](https://github.com/hsr88/mouzi/releases)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/github/license/hsr88/mouzi?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/hsr88/mouzi)

A quiet, automatic file organizer for your desktop. Mouzi watches your Downloads folder - or any folder you choose - and sorts new files into the right places without popups or distractions.

### ✨ Highlights

- 📁 Automatically organizes images, documents, installers, archives, and other files
- ⚙️ Supports custom rules and dynamic placeholders such as `{year}` and `{month}`
- ↩️ Keeps a history of every action with one-click undo
- 🖥️ Runs quietly in the system tray
- 🔒 Works entirely locally - no accounts or cloud uploads

**[View source code →](https://github.com/hsr88/mouzi)**  
**[Visit mouzi.cc →](https://mouzi.cc)**

### 📡 EnvTunnel

[![GitHub stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/hsr88/envtunnel?style=flat-square&logo=github&label=Stars)](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel/stargazers)
[![GitHub release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/hsr88/envtunnel?style=flat-square&label=Release)](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel/releases)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel/blob/main/LICENSE)

A free and open-source, local-first desktop tool that detects active development servers and instantly generates QR codes for them.

Open your localhost project on a phone or tablet with one scan - without manually typing local IP addresses.

- 🔍 Automatically scans popular development ports
- 📱 Generates instant QR codes for active servers
- 🧠 Detects Vite, Next.js, Astro, Angular, Nuxt, and other frameworks
- ➕ Supports custom ports and URL paths
- 🖥️ Runs quietly in the Windows system tray
- 📴 Works completely offline - no accounts, cloud services, or internet required
- 📖 Free and open source under the MIT License

[Source code](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel) · [Download](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel/releases) · [MIT License](https://github.com/hsr88/envtunnel/blob/main/LICENSE)
